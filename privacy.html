Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

SagePlot makes free, data-led content about Singapore property and schools. We collect as little about you as we can.

This website

  • No accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics or tracking scripts. The budget slider runs in your browser and sends nothing to us.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, its fonts come from Google Fonts and its charting library from jsDelivr. Like any web host, these services receive technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the pages; see their own privacy statements.

Messages to us

  • If you message @hellosageplot on Instagram or TikTok, or comment on our posts, we use what you choose to send only to reply to you and, if you ask for one, to run your free one-to-one consultation.
  • We never sell your details, and we do not pass them to property agents or anyone else without your explicit consent.
  • We keep messages only as long as we need them to help you, and delete them when you ask.

Our analytics apps

We use the official YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok APIs only to read statistics about SagePlot's own accounts and posts: counts such as views, likes, shares and followers. These apps do not collect information about the people who watch or follow us, and the statistics are used only to improve our content.

Your choices

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent. Message @hellosageplot on Instagram or TikTok.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this page and its date.
