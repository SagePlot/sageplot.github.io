# P1 Power Move Planner by SagePlot

A free tool: what a family-size home (1,000–1,199 sqft, for a family of six) near Singapore's popular primary schools costs to rent and to buy, from past transactions. One page per school with a map, a budget slider and the condo projects with enough family-size deals, plus a PDF school card.

**Educational content, not financial or property advice. Past transactions, not a recommendation. SagePlot is not a property agent.**
